## Plain lines

1. Imagine you step outside tonight, and the Moon is simply gone. No explosion. It just isn't there. What would change?
2. First, nights would get much darker. The full Moon is by far the brightest thing in the night sky, and many animals time their lives by it.
3. The Moon's gravity pulls on the oceans, raising the tides. Without it, only the Sun would make tides, and they'd be less than half as high.
4. Tide pools, mudflats, and the creatures that live by the rhythm of the tides would all have to adapt, or vanish.
5. The Moon also helps steady Earth's tilt. Without it, over millions of years, our planet could wobble far more, and the seasons could swing to extremes.
6. The Moon has been slowly braking Earth's spin. When the last dinosaurs lived, a day lasted about twenty-three and a half hours. Without the Moon, that slowdown would mostly stop.
7. And there would be no more solar eclipses. Even the word 'month' comes from the Moon.
8. Luckily, the Moon is still here. But it is slowly drifting away from us: about three point eight centimetres every year.
9. So next time you see the Moon, say thanks. It's doing a lot more than lighting up the night.
