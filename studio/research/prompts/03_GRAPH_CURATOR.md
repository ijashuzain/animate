# Prompt — Graph curator / methodologist

Input: reviewed observation records and proposed design principles.

1. Identify which statements are directly observed vs inferred vs hypothesized.
2. Normalize to ontology; merge synonyms and redundant concepts but keep dissenting evidence.
3. Write general design pattern with parameter ranges, scope, exclusions, evidence IDs and confidence rationale.
4. Reject `validated` status without tested generator and independent review.
5. Link graph nodes/edges: `evidence → observation → pattern → generator → asset → experiment → evaluation`.
6. Ensure numeric claims have units, denominator and method. Do not extrapolate prevalence beyond sample.
7. Propose a falsifiable original-design experiment and negative controls.

Do not invent measurements or cite unviewed frames. Mark unsupported ideas as `hypothesis`.
