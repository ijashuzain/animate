## Description

Why do onions make you cry? Cutting an onion sets off a 2-enzyme chemical trap. Plus how to cut onions without crying, and the tearless onion.

Why do onions make you cry? When you cut one, enzymes turn its sulfur chemicals into an irritating gas (syn-propanethial-S-oxide) that drifts up to your eyes, and your tear glands wash it away. Scientists think it's a defence against animals, and a tearless onion, the Sunion, took 30 years of breeding.
