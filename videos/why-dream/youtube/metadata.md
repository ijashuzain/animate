## Description

Why do we dream? You spend about two hours a night in dreams, mostly in REM sleep, and scientists still argue what they are for.

Four ideas, each answering the last "but why?": dreams as the brain stitching random signals into a story, as memory filing, as overnight emotional therapy, and as a survival drill. Nobody knows for certain which is right, but newborn babies, who spend about half their sleep in REM, offer a clue.
