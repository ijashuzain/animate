12. November thirtieth, twenty twenty-two. ChatGPT launches. A million people sign up in five days.

## Facts on screen

- Turing's 1950 paper "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" opens with "Can machines think?"
- The 1956 Dartmouth Summer Research Project (a two-month, ten-researcher study) named the field.
- Rosenblatt's perceptron, 1958.
- ELIZA (Weizenbaum, 1966) matched patterns and reflected the user's words.
- The first AI winter, about 1974–80.
- Backpropagation: Rumelhart, Hinton and Williams, Nature, 1986.
- Deep Blue searched about 200 million positions a second; it beat Kasparov in 1997.
- AlexNet (2012): top-5 error of about 15% against about 26% for the runner-up, trained on two GPUs.
- AlphaGo beat Lee Sedol 4–1 in 2016; commentators first read move 37 of game 2 as a mistake.
- "Attention Is All You Need" (the transformer), 2017.
- GPT-3 (2020) has 175 billion parameters.
- ChatGPT launched on 30 Nov 2022 and passed one million users in about five days.
- Claude Code (2025).
