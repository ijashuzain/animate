# Who killed the dinosaurs? — research

**The one true thing:** the evidence (a worldwide clay layer rich in iridium, shocked quartz, a crater of the same age)
points to an asteroid; volcanoes are a debated second suspect; and birds are surviving dinosaurs.

**Format:** mystery (the case, clue 1-4, suspect 2, the twist, case closed). **Style:** crosshatch ink (a detective's
notebook). **Genre:** mystery. **Transitions:** dip (dark cuts between case beats), zoom (into the clay, the crater),
shutter (look closer at a clue), scatter (the eruption).

## Facts
- K-Pg boundary ~66 million years ago (66.043 +/- 0.011 Ma); ~75% of plant and animal species lost, all non-avian
  dinosaurs (Wikipedia: K-Pg boundary; Alvarez hypothesis).
- 1980: Luis and Walter Alvarez, Frank Asaro, Helen Michel found iridium hundreds of times normal in boundary clay (Science).
- Chicxulub, Yucatan: impactor 10-15 km (2010 review panel); crater ~180 km wide (commonly cited; from knowledge).
- Shocked quartz and glass spherules in the boundary layer (standard K-Pg evidence; from knowledge).
- Deccan Traps eruptions around the same time; their role is debated (from knowledge; hedged).
- Birds are dinosaurs (National Geographic, L. Chiappe); small ground-dwelling birds likely survived (Field et al. 2018).
