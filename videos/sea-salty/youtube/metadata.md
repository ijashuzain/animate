## Description

Why is the sea salty when rivers are fresh? Four clues about where the salt in the ocean comes from: rain, rivers, sea-floor vents and the Sun.

Why is the ocean salty but rivers aren't? Rain dissolves rock, rivers carry the minerals to the sea, hot vents on the sea floor add more, and evaporation lifts the water but leaves the salt behind. The twist: the rivers were never really fresh.

- About 35 g of salt per litre of seawater (average salinity ~35 parts per thousand; stated from knowledge)
