# Why do we dream? — research

**The one true thing:** nobody knows for certain; four main ideas each explain part of it, and babies' REM is a clue.

**Format:** question chain (why? / when? / idea 1-4 / so which? / the clue). **Style:** sketchbook (dreams as doodles in
thought clouds). **Genre:** lo-fi (sleepy, warm). **Transitions:** cloud shape (into the dream), page turn (each idea is a
page), ink bleed (feelings), fade.

## Facts
- About two hours of dreaming a night; REM periods grow from ~10 min to up to ~1 hour across the night (Sleep Foundation).
- Most vivid dreams in REM; eyes dart, brain active, body kept still (REM atonia; textbook, stated from knowledge).
- Theories: activation-synthesis (Hobson & McCarley), memory consolidation (best supported), emotional processing,
  threat simulation (Revonsuo) (Lumen Learning, Introduction to Psychology). Scientists don't know for certain (Healthline).
- Newborns spend about half their sleep in REM, adults about a fifth (standard sleep-medicine figures; from knowledge);
  developmental idea that REM helps build neural pathways (Healthline).
