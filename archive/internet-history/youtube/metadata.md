# The Internet's First Word Was "LO", Then It Crashed | A Short History of the Internet

## Title

*Alternatives:* "How the Internet Began: From "LO" to 5.5 Billion People" · "The Internet in 2 Minutes"

## Description

In 1969, two computers tried to say "login". They crashed after two letters: L, O.

This 2-minute animated history follows Pip, a tiny envelope, from that first glitch to the 5.5 billion people online today: email and the @ sign, TCP/IP packets, the World Wide Web, search, social networks and the smartphone.
