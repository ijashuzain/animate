# What if Earth stopped spinning? — research

**The one true thing:** the spin shapes almost everything (speed, day length, ocean shape, maybe the magnetic field),
and Earth really is slowing, but only by about 2 ms per century.

**Format:** what if (time tags: right now, 1 second later, 1 minute later, day 1, year 1). **Style:** riso (a bold printed
world, space + globe read well as plate tones). **Genre:** ambient (wonder, scale). **Transitions:** flash (the sudden
stop), ink (oceans surging), dip (into the long night), spin (the planet turning).

## Facts
- Equator moves east ~1,670-1,674 km/h (ScienceAlert). Jet airliner cruise ~900 km/h (common knowledge).
- Sudden stop: air, water, people keep moving; poles least affected; tsunamis, earthquakes (Space.com, A. Layden).
- Gradual stop: about 6 months of day and 6 of night (Space.com, Layden).
- Equatorial bulge ~21 km (6,378 vs 6,357 km radius; stated from knowledge). Without spin the oceans migrate to the poles,
  one equatorial megacontinent; Greenland and Canada submerged (Esri/W. Fraczek model via Popular Science, IFLScience).
- The magnetic field could be lost/weaken without rotation (IFLScience; hedged "some scientists think").
- Day lengthens ~2 ms per century; ~400 million years ago a day was ~22 hours (coral growth rings, ~400 days a year).
