## Description

You step outside tonight, and the Moon is simply gone. No explosion, just gone. What would change?

Darker nights, much smaller tides, struggling shorelines, a wobbling planet, a day that stops getting longer, and no more eclipses. Then the good news, and the twist: the Moon is slowly drifting away from us.
