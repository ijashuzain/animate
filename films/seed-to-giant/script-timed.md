# Seed to Giant: voice-over script (record after the picture; each line must land in its window)

Pace: about 2.6 words a second, warm and wondering, a small smile on "Past a blue whale standing on its tail."
Leave the pauses: the picture has a gag in each one.

| window | line | words |
|---|---|---|
| 0.3 - 4.4 s | A giant sequoia starts as a seed about the size of a grain of rice. | 14 |
| 4.7 - 9.4 s | It can wait in a closed cone for twenty years, until a fire's heat pops it open. | 16 |
| 9.8 - 13.9 s | It spins down onto fresh ash, and sends one root into the ground. | 13 |
| 14.1 - 15.8 s | Then it climbs. | 3 |
| 16.3 - 22.8 s | Past you. (16.4) Past your house. (18.1) Past a blue whale standing on its tail. (20.2) | 13 |
| 23.0 - 27.8 s | Two thousand years later, it's as tall as a twenty-seven-storey building. | 12 |
| 28.0 - 29.8 s | All from a seed the size of rice. | 8 |

Export the voice as one WAV/MP3 from 0.0 s (or one file per line named 01.wav ... 07.wav) and drop it in
films/seed-to-giant/audio/: the build places it on the timeline and lowers the music under it.
