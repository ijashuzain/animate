# Seed to Giant (30 s pilot): the plan

## The idea (picked by the user from 3 pitches, 2026-10-21)
**One continuous shot, no cuts.** The camera rides with a giant sequoia's life: first DOWN with the falling seed (from a
cone high in a burning forest to the ash), into the soil with the first root, then it turns and rides UP with the
growing tip for the rest of the film, past things for scale, until it breaks through the clouds. A fall, then a rise.

## Reference look
Kurzgesagt-inspired flat vector: no outlines, rich gradients, soft glows and rim light, saturated colour, depth from
atmosphere (far layers paler and bluer), many small details (dust, embers, leaves, birds). The colour arc follows the
journey: night-orange fire -> deep soil browns -> morning blues -> gold above the clouds.

## Pacing
RETENTION, not teaching: a new thing every 1-2 s, the camera never stops, one line = one gag. 30 s, ~79 words.

## Facts (each with a source)
| claim | source |
|---|---|
| seeds are small: about 1/8 to 1/4 inch long and wide including the wing (~3-6 mm; a grain of rice is ~5-7 mm long) | John Muir quoted by NPS (npshistory.com/series/science/hartesveldt) ; NPS germination study: seeds ~4-8 mm (nps.gov/parkhistory/online_books/science/12/chap5.htm) |
| cones are (semi-)serotinous and may stay green and closed for over twenty years; fire's heat dries them and releases seeds | NPS science series ch. 5 and 10; UC ANR "Postfire giant sequoia considerations" (2026) |
| seedlings do best on bare mineral soil / ash after fire | NPS science series ch. 10; Fire journal 2024 (doaj.org/article/c4a599a445ae43938281191d8aa2117c) |
| General Sherman: 83.8 m tall (~27 storeys at ~3.1 m/storey), estimated 2,300-2,700 years old (NPS: at least 2,200) | Wikipedia "General Sherman Tree"; Secret Los Angeles citing NPS |
| a blue whale is up to ~30 m long | stated from knowledge |
Hedges in the voice: "about the size of a grain of rice", "can wait ... twenty years", "two thousand years later".

## Sound
Music: a single build that follows the camera (soft plucks underground, strings and pulse rising, a bright swell and a
hit at the cloud break, a calm chord at the end). SFX on every action: crackle, pop of the cone, whoosh of the fall,
soft thud on ash, a dig, a sprout "fwip", whooshes past each scale object, a whale groan, a wind gust at the clouds.
