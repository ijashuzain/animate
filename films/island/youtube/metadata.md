**Description:**
How are islands formed? How was Hawaii formed? Watch volcanic island formation from a crack in the sea floor, five kilometres down, to a green island with people on its beach: a million years in two minutes.

This island is about half a million years old, and it started in the dark. A plume of hot rock rises from the mantle, lava cools into pillows on the sea floor, the mountain climbs for hundreds of thousands of years, explodes through the waves, and life arrives on wind, waves and wings. Then the sea floor carries it away from the hot spot, and the next island starts to grow.
