# Why Cats Always Land on Their Feet: the plan

## The idea (the user picked "inside the cat"; direct, no control room)
A real cat falls, shown in slow motion with a real timer (milliseconds), and a clean anatomical cutaway glides over its body
at each step: the inner ear's balance organs, the nerve signal to the brain, the eyes, the flexible spine (thoracic vs
lumbar), the legs. The fall repeats from three angles (side, front, top) to show each move. Hook: a cat on a shelf knocks a
cup, falls, and lands; "that took less than a third of a second. Let's slow it down."

## Look
Flat vector, warm living room, a ginger cat drawn after the user's references (to be requested). Cutaway: the body turns
translucent and the inner parts glow in clean colours (ear canals blue, nerve yellow, spine cream).
