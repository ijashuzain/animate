## Package
- Title (49): Why Cats Always Land on Their Feet (in Slow Motion)
- Thumbnail words: 0.3 SECONDS
- Description: A cat slips off a bookcase and lands on its feet in about a third of a second. We slow it down and look
  inside: the balance organ in the ear, the signal to the legs, the bendy spine that lets the front and back halves turn
  one after the other, and why kittens need a few weeks to learn it.
  Sources: Higurashi et al. (The Anatomical Record, 2026) on the front/back rotation and spine flexibility; overviews of the
