# The 10-Day Trip of a Single Raindrop: the plan

## The idea (picked by the user from 3 pitches, 2026-10-21): Drip's road trip
One water molecule, **Drip** (an oxygen-ball body with a face, its two hydrogen atoms for ears), takes the whole water
cycle as a road trip. A **passport** pops up at each stop and gets a stamp with how long water stays there. The camera
travels with Drip; scale changes are camera moves (zoom into a drop, pull out of a cloud), never plain cuts.

## Reference look
Kurzgesagt-like flat vector, rounder and bubblier than the pilot: soft gradients, glows, bubbles, no outlines.
Colour arc: rain-grey window -> deep ocean teal -> sunlit turquoise surface -> pink-violet cold sky -> storm indigo ->
green-gold mountain -> turquoise river -> sepia history (the twist) -> golden sunset sea. Font: Baloo 2 (labels, stamps).

## Pacing
RETENTION: a new gag every 1-2 s, ~2.6 words/s, 117 s. One 2.5 s silence before the twist.

## Facts (each with a source)
| claim | source |
|---|---|
| water stays in the atmosphere about 9 days on average (other estimates ~11) | Hawaii GG325 lecture notes (soest.hawaii.edu/krubin/GG325/lect15.pdf); Encyclopedia.com "Water cycle" |
| about 97% of Earth's water is in the oceans; ocean residence time ~3,000+ years (estimates 10^3-10^4) | USGS Water Science School; Iowa State reservoir table (Graedel & Crutzen 1993); Encyclopedia.com |
| glaciers/ice caps hold water ~10 to 1,000 years (we say "decades"); groundwater: centuries (shallow 100-200 y, deep much longer) | Iowa State table; Spokane Aquifer "Residence time of groundwater" |
| rivers renew their water in about two weeks (~16 days) | Iowa State table (~2 weeks); Harvard BioNumbers 115098 |
| cloud droplets form on tiny particles of dust, salt or smoke | USGS "Precipitation and the water cycle" |
| cloud droplets fall about 1 cm/s; raindrops ~4-9 m/s | UW Atmos 101 lecture 21; Hawaii atmo chapter 7 |
| a raindrop holds roughly a million cloud droplets' worth of water | derived: raindrop r ~2 mm vs cloud droplet r ~10-20 µm -> (100-200)^3 = 10^6-10^7 |
| raindrops are not tear-shaped: small ones are spheres, larger ones look like hamburger buns | USGS "Are raindrops shaped like teardrops?"; NASA GPM |
| water molecules cycle for billions of years; your water "could have" passed through a dinosaur | stated from knowledge (Earth's water is ~4 billion years old; hedged "could have") |

## Sound

ocean, bright marimba at the surface, airy bells in the cloud, rain patter rhythm, a bouncy bass for the river slide);
silence; a warm string swell for the twist; the theme returns at sunset. SFX: rain on glass, bubbles, whale call,
jiggle rattles, boing launch, whoosh up, chattering teeth, crystal clink, bumper "boop" merges, falling whistle, splat,
signpost creak, slide whoosh, waterwheel, splash, page flips, stamp thumps, dinosaur gulp.
