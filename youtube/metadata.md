## 2. Why Is the Sky Blue?

**Title:**
Why Is the Sky Blue? (And Why Sunsets Are Red) | Explained in 2 Minutes

*Shorter alternatives:* "Why the Sky Is Blue, Explained Simply" · "The Sky Should Be Violet. Here's Why It Isn't."

**Description:**

Same sky, same Sun. So why blue at noon and red at sunset?

Follow Ray, a little sunbeam, as he splits into his colour siblings. Light is every colour mixed together, each colour is a wave, and the air bounces short blue waves all over the sky. You'll also find out why the sky isn't violet, why sunsets turn red, and why on Mars it's the other way around.

⏱️ Chapters
0:00 The question
0:12 White light is every colour
0:25 Colours are waves
0:38 The air scatters blue
0:52 Blue from every direction
1:04 Why not violet?
1:17 Why sunsets are red
1:33 On Mars, it's flipped
1:47 Summary

📚 The science: Rayleigh scattering. Scattering is far stronger for short wavelengths (it goes with 1/wavelength⁴), so blue (~450 nm) scatters several times more than red (~700 nm). Blue sunsets on Mars have been photographed by NASA's rovers.

🎨 Animated entirely in code, in a cut-paper style.

## 3. What If the Moon Disappeared?

**Title:**
What If the Moon Disappeared Tonight?

*Alternatives:* "What Would Happen If the Moon Vanished?" · "Life Without the Moon: 6 Things That Would Change"

**Description:**

You step outside tonight, and the Moon is simply gone. No explosion, just gone. What would change?

Darker nights, much smaller tides, struggling shorelines, a wobbling planet, a day that stops getting longer, and no more eclipses. Then the good news, and the twist: the Moon is slowly drifting away from us.

📚 Sources
- NASA, "Measuring the Moon's Distance": lunar laser ranging off the Apollo retroreflectors, about 3.8 cm a year
- The Sun's tidal pull on Earth is about 46% of the Moon's
- Day length about 70 million years ago (~23.5 h): growth bands in fossil shells (de Winter et al., 2020)

## Upload tips
- **Voice first:** upload once your narration is mixed in. The silent pauses before each payoff (about 2.5 s) are deliberate.
- **Shorts:** a 30–60 s vertical cut of each video can be rendered from the same source (ask for it).
- **Accessibility:** add captions (`narration.srt` from the render, adjusted to your actual takes).
